01

Getting started

Set up the app in the right order so balances and reports begin accurately.

  1. Create your account, confirm your email, and sign in.
  2. Add every bank, digital wallet, and Cash on Hand account that currently holds available money.
  3. Enter each account’s current balance as its opening balance. Opening balances are not treated as income.
  4. Add existing loans and credit cards before recording transactions connected to them.
  5. Record new income and expenses from their actual transaction dates.
Good to know

Use current balances—not lifetime deposits—as opening balances.

02

Navigating the app

Understand the four main areas and the Add button.

  1. Overview shows Total Available Funds, savings, accounts, projections, spending, debts, and recent activity.
  2. Under Spending by category, select any category to show or hide every expense included in its total.
  3. Under Debt Overview, select Loans or Credit Cards to inspect each account, then choose View all for the full debt workspace.
  4. Activity contains searchable transaction history, date filters, recurring schedules, and expense details.
  5. Debts contains separate Loans and Credit Cards views.
  6. Premium displays the planned subscription options and future premium tools.
  7. Use the Add button to record income or an expense from any main screen.
03

Accounts and Cash on Hand

Track money held in banks, wallets, and physical cash.

  1. From Overview, select Add account.
  2. Choose Bank or digital wallet, or Cash on Hand.
  3. For a bank or wallet, enter an account name, provider, and available balance.
  4. For Cash on Hand, enter the physical cash currently available.
  5. Select the pencil icon to change an account name, provider, or current balance.
Good to know

A balance edit creates a visible Account balance adjustment but does not count as income or expense.

An account with linked transactions cannot be deleted until those links are removed or moved.

04

Recording income

Add income to the correct destination account and optionally reserve savings.

  1. Select Add, then choose Income.
  2. Enter the amount, source or description, category, and transaction date.
  3. Select the account that received the money.
  4. If needed, create a new account or Cash on Hand directly from the income form.
  5. Optionally enter a savings allocation and choose the account where the savings will be held.
  6. Review the information and save the income.
Good to know

Only an amount specifically entered as a savings allocation is counted as reserved savings.

05

Recording expenses

Charge every expense to the account or credit card that actually paid for it.

  1. Select Add, then choose Expense.
  2. Enter the amount, description, category, and transaction date.
  3. Select the account or credit card charged.
  4. If the account is not listed, choose an option under Create a new account to add a bank, digital wallet, Cash on Hand, or credit card without leaving the expense form.
  5. For a credit-card purchase, the due date is calculated from the selected card’s statement and due-day settings.
  6. For other expenses, add a due date when the payment is still upcoming.
  7. Choose One-time or a recurring schedule, then save.
Good to know

Account-funded expenses reduce Total Available Funds immediately.

Credit-card purchases increase card utilization and reduce available credit without reducing Total Available Funds until payment.

06

Recurring expenses and subscriptions

Schedule monthly, quarterly, or annual expenses without manually re-entering them.

  1. While adding an expense, choose Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
  2. Set the start date and, if applicable, an end date.
  3. The schedule appears under Automatic Expenses in Activity.
  4. Use the pencil icon to edit future occurrences. Existing transaction history is not changed.
  5. Use the trash icon to stop a non-installment schedule.
Good to know

Recurring items are included in Projected Disposable Income based on their applicable transaction or credit-card due date.

Installment schedules stop automatically after the final installment and cannot be deleted early because the full card obligation is already booked.

07

Credit cards

Monitor credit limit, available credit, utilization, purchases, installments, and payments.

  1. Open Debts, choose Credit Cards, and select Add card.
  2. Enter the card name, issuer, credit limit, current balance, statement balance, minimum payment, statement day, and due day.
  3. Record purchases as expenses and select the correct credit card as the charged account.
  4. For an installment purchase, enter the monthly installment, start date, and number of installments from 1 to 60.
  5. The app calculates the installment end date and books the full obligation to the card balance.
Good to know

Available credit and utilization update when the purchase is recorded—not when the card bill is paid.

The days-to-spend sticker counts down to the next statement closing day and changes color when the cutoff is near.

08

Loans

Track remaining balances, monthly repayments, and upcoming due dates.

  1. Open Debts, choose Loans, and select Add loan.
  2. Enter the loan name, lender, original amount, current balance, monthly payment, and next due date.
  3. The overview shows progress and the next monthly repayment.
  4. Overdue and upcoming installments are included in the disposable-income projection.
  5. Confirmed full monthly payments advance the next due date when a balance remains.
09

Paying loans and credit cards

Record the cash movement and reconcile the debt balance correctly.

  1. For a manual or partial payment, add an Expense and choose Bills Payment.
  2. Select the account providing the money, then link the payment to the correct loan or credit card.
  3. Open the related loan or card and confirm the pending payment.
  4. For a due-date prompt, select Pay now, choose the source account, and confirm.
  5. A full payment clears the due notification; a partial payment leaves the remaining amount visible.
Good to know

Debt repayments reduce cash and debt balances but are excluded from spending totals to prevent double-counting.

10

Projected Disposable Income

Estimate what remains after savings reserves and obligations through a selected date.

  1. On Overview, choose the Project through date. It defaults to the last day of the current month.
  2. The calculation begins with Total Available Funds.
  3. Current reserved savings and all upcoming obligations—including loan payments, card dues, and recurring expenses—are deducted once.
  4. Select View included expense details to inspect each amount and due date used.
  5. Paid and partially paid card dues are reconciled so only the remaining due is projected.
Good to know

Loan payments appear inside Upcoming & recurring expenses. Matching loan schedules are consolidated so they are not counted twice.

A negative result means scheduled obligations are greater than currently available disposable funds.

11

Activity and transaction history

Find, review, edit, and understand recorded money movements.

  1. Open Activity to view all transactions from newest to oldest.
  2. Search by description or category.
  3. Use From and To dates to filter an inclusive transaction period.
  4. Select Expenses in the summary to see totals by category and individual expense details.
  5. Use the pencil icon to review and edit an entry, or the trash icon to delete it.
Good to know

Opening balances and account balance adjustments remain visible but do not count as income or expenses.

12

Due-date alerts

Review upcoming and overdue obligations when opening the app.

  1. Select the bell icon at the top of the app.
  2. Choose an advance reminder period of 3, 7, or 14 days.
  3. Review overdue, due-today, and upcoming loan, card, and expense reminders.
  4. Open the related debt overview to record or confirm payment.
Good to know

Current reminders appear inside the app; email reminders require a separate transactional email service.

13

Editing and deleting safely

Understand how corrections affect linked balances.

  1. Select the pencil icon beside an account, transaction, loan, card, or recurring schedule.
  2. Review the proposed revisions before confirming the change.
  3. Linked credit-card purchases and confirmed payments reconcile their associated debt balance.
  4. Deleting a confirmed payment restores the linked debt balance when possible.
  5. The app blocks deletion when it could disconnect an account, card, loan, or installment schedule from linked records.
14

Profile, security, and account deletion

Manage your profile and permanently remove your data when needed.

  1. Select your profile photo or initials to open Account settings.
  2. Upload or remove a profile photo, open legal policies, visit this Help Center, or contact support.
  3. Use Sign out when leaving a shared device.
  4. To permanently delete the account, open Danger Zone, choose Delete account, and type DELETE.
Good to know

Account deletion permanently removes app records and sign-in access and cannot be undone.

15

Troubleshooting

Resolve common entry, balance, and sign-in problems.

  1. If an expense cannot be saved, confirm an account or credit card was selected and has enough available funds or credit.
  2. If an account shows Insufficient funds, review its linked recurring expenses, payments, and adjustments.
  3. If a card balance looks incorrect, check that each purchase used the correct card and each payment was confirmed only once.
  4. If a confirmation email does not arrive, check spam and verify that the address was entered correctly.
  5. For a reconciliation warning, do not repeat the transaction. Contact support with the date, amount, and account name.
