YOUR DATA, EXPLAINED

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 20, 2026

Pinoy Pocket Budget is a personal budgeting and debt-tracking service operated by its individual owner in the Philippines. This policy explains what information we process, why we need it, and the choices available to you.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Pinoy Pocket Budget acts as the personal information controller for information collected through this service. Privacy questions, access requests, corrections, objections, portability requests, and deletion requests may be sent to support@pinoypocketbudget.app.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, optional profile photo, account identifier, authentication status, and account timestamps.
  • Budget information you provide: income and expense amounts, categories, descriptions, and dates.
  • Loan information you provide: loan and lender names, original amount, remaining balance, monthly payment, and due date.
  • Credit-card tracking information you provide: a card nickname, issuer, credit limit, balances, minimum payment, statement day, and due day.
  • Technical and security information: authentication events, error and security logs, device or browser information, and network information that may be recorded by our service providers.
  • Support information: the content of messages you send when requesting help or exercising a privacy right.

Please do not enter full card numbers, security codes, bank passwords, government identification numbers, or other information the app does not request. Pinoy Pocket Budget does not connect directly to your bank or card account.

3. How and why we use information

We process information to create and secure your account, provide budgeting and debt-tracking features, store and synchronize your entries, send account confirmation and password-recovery emails, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, protect the service, and comply with applicable law.

Our legal bases may include your consent, steps needed to provide the service you request, compliance with legal obligations, and legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, as permitted by the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and its implementing rules.

4. Service providers and data transfers

We use service providers to operate the app, including Supabase for authentication and database services, an email-delivery provider for authentication messages, and hosting and domain infrastructure providers. These providers process information on our behalf according to their services and safeguards. Their systems may process or store information outside the Philippines.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal budget entries with advertisers. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or in connection with a legitimate business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards and notice.

5. Storage, security, and retention

Account access is protected through authentication, and database access controls are designed to separate each user's records. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

We retain account and budget information while your account is active and as reasonably necessary to provide the service, resolve disputes, protect against abuse, or meet legal obligations. When information is no longer necessary, we will delete or anonymize it where reasonably practicable.

6. Cookies and local storage

The app and its service providers may use cookies or browser storage that are necessary to keep you signed in, maintain security, remember essential settings, and operate the service. We do not currently use this information for targeted advertising.

7. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to be informed, access your data, object to certain processing, correct inaccurate information, request erasure or blocking, obtain portable data, file a complaint, and seek damages where allowed. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Signed-in users can permanently delete their account and associated profile, transactions, loans, and credit-card tracking information from Account Settings. You may also send a request to support@pinoypocketbudget.app. You may contact or file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

8. Children

The service is intended for people at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will post the updated version here, change the effective date, and provide additional notice when a material change requires it.
